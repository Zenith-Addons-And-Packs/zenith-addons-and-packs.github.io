Zenith Ability Weapons

Seven custom weapons with real abilities for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, by Zenith. Each one has hand-drawn pixel art, custom particles, upgrade levels, a cooldown bar and settings you tune in-game. They're built to run smoothly on busy servers.

Use the weapon (right-click, or hold on mobile) to fire its ability. The cooldown shows on the hotbar slot like an ender pearl's, and as a bar on the action bar (■■■■■□□□□□ 4.2s) while you hold the weapon. A soft ping tells you when it's ready again.

Upgrades (levels I–V)

Every weapon can be upgraded from I up to V (admins can raise the cap to X).

  • Each level adds +12% power (damage, radius, effect duration) and -6% cooldown.
  • The level is saved on the item itself. It shows in the name (e.g. Dash Blade III) and in the lore with a level bar. Trade it, drop it, store it: it stays upgraded.
  • Cost goes up each level. With Zenith Economy installed you pay in money (750, 1,500, 2,250, 3,000 by default; uses Economy's scoreboard mirror, which is on by default). Without it you pay in XP levels (5, 10, 15, 20). Admins can force XP.
  • Upgrade from the Armory, from a weapon's page, or with /zenith_aw:upgrade. The menu lists every ability weapon in your inventory, held one first, with the before/after stats and the price.

Feedback

  • Cooldown bar on the action bar while you hold a weapon that's recharging, plus a ready ping. Each player can turn either off for themselves in the Armory (My HUD).
  • Hit confirmation: a rising chime and Hit 3 on the action bar when your ability lands.
  • Kill effects: a burst in your effect color and a bell when an ability kill lands, KO on the action bar.
  • Ability kill streaks: a small title at 2, 3, 5, 7 and 10 player kills (then every 5). Never more than one every 2 seconds. With Zenith FFA it waits for FFA's own kill title instead of covering it.

Leaderboard

Casts, player kills and mob kills per weapon, per player. Open it with /zenith_aw:stats or from the Armory. A kill counts for a weapon when it lands within a few seconds of that weapon's ability hit (so burning and poison kills count). Players in a Zenith Duel or Event don't add to the stats.

Customization

Everything is set in-game from the admin panel (Forge, /zenith_aw:forge) and saved to the world. No files to edit.

Per weapon:

  • On/off
  • Cooldown (1–60s)
  • Power 25–200%: damage, range and effect duration
  • Damage multiplier 25–300%: damage only
  • Effect color (11 presets or the weapon's own)
  • Give to any player, at any level
  • Reset to defaults

Settings:

  • Activation: plain use, or sneak + use
  • Abilities hit players and/or mobs
  • Clan-safe (on by default): abilities skip your clanmates and allied clans from Zenith Clans, including fire, poison, freeze and knockback
  • No-ability radius around the world spawn
  • Let players pick their own effect color
  • Switch hints

Upgrades: on/off, max level (2–10), power and cooldown per level, pay with Economy money or XP, cost per level, and set the level of the weapon you're holding.

No-ability zones: stand somewhere, tap Add a zone here, pick a radius. Nobody can use abilities inside, and nobody inside can be hit by them (blink and lightning can't target a zone either). Zones cover all heights. Up to 50 zones. Players tagged zenith_aw:noability can't use abilities anywhere.

Feedback: cooldown bar, ready ping, hit confirmation and streak titles, each on/off for the whole server.

Players (Armory, /zenith_aw:armory): browse every weapon with its ability and stats, upgrade weapons, see the leaderboard, pick a personal effect color, and set their own HUD.

Optimization

  • No per-tick loops. Abilities run on events. The only timers run for players with a cooldown or stance active, a few times a second.
  • Native cooldowns: the game's own hotbar cooldown, with an action-bar bar only while you hold the weapon.
  • Particle quality Low / Medium / High: scales every effect's particle count and trail density. Use Low for big servers or low-end phones.
  • Server-wide cast cap (default 30 casts/second) stops ability spam from lagging the server.
  • Abilities that find no target (no room to blink, nothing to strike) fail without using the cooldown.
  • Stats are kept in memory and saved in batches.
  • Every effect is tinted from 2 tiny shared textures. No huge particle files.

Works with the other Zenith packs

Install them together and these appear automatically:

  • Ranked Duels: a new Abilities ladder (Dash Blade + Inferno Sword + Frost Scythe) with its own Elo. Abilities can't fire during the frozen countdown. Duel kits are always level I, so ranked stays fair.
  • Economy: an Ability Weapons shop category ($1,500–$3,200), and upgrades are paid in Economy money.
  • FFA: four weapons in the coin shop, plus a gold burst on every kill. The FFA menu links to the Armory.
  • Clans: clan-safe abilities (see Customization).
  • Ability damage counts as your hit, so FFA kills, streaks, bounties and Duels wins all work. FFA's safe zone still protects players.

Without this pack installed, those extras are hidden.

Commands

Command Who What
/zenith_aw:armory Everyone Browse weapons, upgrade, leaderboard, effect color, HUD
/zenith_aw:upgrade Everyone Upgrade an ability weapon in your inventory
/zenith_aw:stats Everyone Leaderboard per weapon
/zenith_aw:forge Admins Tune, give, settings, upgrades, zones, feedback, optimization
/zenith_aw:give <players> <weapon> [level] Operators, command blocks, NPCs Give a weapon (e.g. dash_blade 3)
/zenith_aw:setadmin <players> <true|false> Operators Give/remove admin

Weapons are also in the creative inventory (Equipment tab).
