Zenith Clans

Clans: roles, invites, clan chat, home and bank, levels, wars, allies, quests, no friendly fire and leaderboards.

Clans for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, by Zenith. Players found a clan with a name, a colored [TAG] and a home, then level it up together: kills, server events and weekly quests earn clan points, levels unlock perks, and leaders can challenge rival clans to timed wars or team up with allies. Everything runs from one menu, with no command blocks and no experiments.
Version 1.0.0
Experiments None needed. Turn the pack on and play.
Game 1.26.50+
Works on Realms, single-player, hosted servers / BDS
Resource pack Not needed
Namespace zenith_clans

Features

Clans

  • Create a clan with a name, a 2–4 letter [TAG] and a tag color. The tag shows above members' heads.
  • Roles: Leader, Officers and Members. Officers invite, kick members and set the home. The leader promotes, demotes and can hand the clan over.
  • Invites by menu or command, with a timer. A leader who leaves hands the clan to the oldest officer.
  • Member list with online status, roles, kills for the clan and "last seen" for offline members.
  • Clan chat (/zenith_clans:c <message>), a message of the day, and a clan home with a stand-still warmup that cancels if you move or get hit.
  • Clan bank (with Zenith Economy): members deposit coins, the leader withdraws (officers too, if you allow it).

Clan levels

  • Every clan point a clan earns is also XP, and XP never goes down. Clans level up from 1 to 10 (you choose the max).
  • Perks per level: more member slots, a bigger bank, and a shorter home warmup.
  • A colored level badge (Lv1 to Lv10) shows in menus, leaderboards and nametags.
  • Level-ups get a big on-screen title and a perks summary for every online member.

Clan wars

  • A leader challenges another clan and their leader accepts. For 15 minutes (you choose), the clan that earns more clan points wins.
  • Kills between the two clans give extra points, so fighting each other matters most.
  • Live score, time left and a progress bar in the war menu, plus warnings at 5 minutes and 1 minute left.
  • The result is announced to the whole server. The winner gets bonus points, and every clan keeps a win/loss record.
  • A cooldown between wars keeps it fair.

Allies

  • Up to 2 allied clans per clan (you choose). Leaders send and accept ally requests.
  • No friendly fire between allies (can be turned off). Allies are listed on every clan's info card.

Weekly quests

  • A new set of quests every Monday (00:00 UTC), the same for every clan. Every member's progress counts.
  • Quests come from a pool: get kills, earn clan points, deposit coins in the bank, earn points in server events, win a clan war. Quests that need an addon you don't have are left out.
  • Progress bars in the clan menu, and clan points plus a title when a quest is done.

Fair play

  • No friendly fire between clanmates. Hits are blocked, not healed back.
  • Kills in Zenith Ranked Duels and Zenith Events don't count for clan points, so rematches can't be farmed. Clanmates can still fight each other there.
  • Killing the same player again within 2 minutes gives no points (you choose the time).
  • Clan home can't be used during a duel or event.

Leaderboards

  • In-game boards with pages: Top Clans, Highest Level, Most Kills, Best K/D, War Wins and Biggest Clans. Tap any clan to see its info card and members.
  • Floating leaderboards you place anywhere. They update live.

All data survives /reload, relogs and restarts, and works for offline members too.

Setup (1 minute)

  1. Turn on the pack (Behavior Packs → Zenith Clans).
  2. As an operator: /zenith_clans:setadmin @s true
  3. /zenith_clans:admin:
    • Settings: member slots, points, levels, wars, allies and quests.
    • Floating leaderboards: place a board or two.
  4. Done. Players use /zenith_clans:menu.

Commands

Command Who What
/zenith_clans:menu Everyone One menu for everything: create, members, quests, wars, allies, level, home, bank
/zenith_clans:c <message> Members Clan chat (no message opens a chat box)
/zenith_clans:invite <player> Leaders, officers Invite a player
/zenith_clans:accept · :deny Everyone Answer a clan invite
/zenith_clans:home Members Teleport to the clan home (warmup)
/zenith_clans:members Members Member list with online status and roles
/zenith_clans:quests Members This week's quests and progress
/zenith_clans:war Members War score, challenges, declare war (leaders)
/zenith_clans:allies Members Allies and ally requests (leaders manage them)
/zenith_clans:info [tag or name] Everyone Info card for your clan or another
/zenith_clans:top Everyone Clan leaderboards
/zenith_clans:admin Admins Admin panel
/zenith_clans:setadmin <players> <true|false> Operators Give/remove admin

For command blocks and NPCs: /scriptevent zenith_clans:points <amount>, run as a player, adds clan points to that player's clan (a negative amount takes them away).

Admin panel

Settings (33 options in 5 groups) · Manage clans (give or take points, set level, rename, call off a war, disband) · Clan wars (live scores, call off) · Floating leaderboards (place, remove nearest) · New season (reset stats, with or without levels).

Customization

Everything is set in-game from the admin panel and saved to the world. No files to edit.

Group Settings
Clans & bank Member slots at level 1, invite time, home warmup, creation cost, clan bank on/off, officers can withdraw, [TAG] nametags
Combat No friendly fire, points per kill (clan player / clanless player), points lost on death, repeat-kill time
Levels Max level, XP per level, extra member slots per level, bank limit and extra space per level, shorter home warmup per level
Allies & wars Allies per clan (0 = off), no friendly fire between allies, wars on/off, war length, war kill bonus, winner bonus, time between wars
Weekly quests Quests on/off, quests per week, reward per quest, target for each quest type

Works with other Zenith addons

Every addon works on its own. Together they connect automatically, with nothing to set up:

  • Zenith Economy: clan bank, a coin cost to create a clan, and the "deposit coins" weekly quest.
  • Zenith FFA: nametags show the clan [TAG], and the FFA menu has a Clans button.
  • Zenith Ranked Duels: nametags show the clan [TAG]. Duel kills don't count for clan points.
  • Zenith Events: event rewards can pay clan points to the winners' clans, and the "earn points in server events" weekly quest appears.
  • Other Zenith addons can tell clanmates and allies apart, because every member carries their clan and ally tags.

Notes

  • When Zenith FFA or Zenith Ranked Duels is installed, that addon draws the nametags (with the clan [TAG]) and Clans leaves them alone.
  • Floating leaderboards are a pack entity (zenith_clans:text). If one doesn't appear, reopen the world once after adding the pack.
  • War and ally requests stay open for 5 minutes. Weekly quests reset every Monday at 00:00 UTC.
