Features

Ranked, the way it should be

Fair matchmaking, arenas that never break, and a reason to keep climbing.

⚔️Ladders with their own Elo

Sword, Axe, UHC, Boxing (first to 100 hits) and Sumo, plus an Abilities ladder with Zenith Ability Weapons.

📈Bronze → Zenith

Six ranks with rank-up announcements. New players place fast with a higher K for their first 10 games.

🎯Smart matchmaking

Starts at ±100 Elo and widens while you wait, so matches are fair and queues stay short.

🔁Self-resetting arenas

Save a snapshot once and every block, lava pool and dropped item is restored after each round.

🛡️Anti-boosting

Playing the same opponent over and over stops earning Elo. No more win-trading.

🗓️Seasons with rewards

Run seasons on a timer or end them yourself. The top 3 of every ladder are archived and paid, then everyone soft-resets.

🏟️Best of 1, 3 or 5

Players pick per challenge. Frozen countdown, round scores, a time limit that only counts fighting time.

📋Match recap

Hits, damage dealt and taken, longest combo and fight time after every match, plus a live combo counter.

💰Wagers & payouts

Coins for wins, an optional ranked entry fee and wagers on duel requests, winner takes both. Works with Zenith Economy.

🎒Kit editor

Arrange every ladder's kit once. Layouts adapt automatically when you change a kit.

👀Watch & duel

Watch any match live, and get your items back when you leave. Challenge friends to unranked duels.

🛡️Clans built in

Clan tags show in nametags, and ranked wins earn points for your Zenith Clans clan.

📊Live Elo boards

Floating leaderboards per ladder, plus profiles with match history and past seasons.

🧭Lobby hotbar

Ranked, Unranked, Kit Editor, Stats and Leaderboards, one tap each. No commands to remember.

🎮Admin panel

Lobby, arenas, ladder kits, settings, live matches and seasons, all in-game.

Setup

Live in 5 minutes

Install

Open the .mcaddon and turn on Zenith Ranked Duels. No experiments.

Lobby & arenas

/zenith_duels:admin: set the lobby, create an arena, and set spawn A and B.

Snapshot

Mark the arena box and press Save snapshot. It resets after every round from now on.

Commands

Short list, all you need

/zenith_duels:menuEverything in one menu (or use the lobby hotbar)
/zenith_duels:rankedPick a ladder and queue
/zenith_duels:duel <player>Challenge a friend (unranked)
/zenith_duels:watchPick a live match to watch
/zenith_duels:recapYour last match recap
/zenith_duels:kitKit layout editor
/zenith_duels:stats [player]Elo, W/L, rank and match history
/zenith_duels:forfeitGive up the current match
/zenith_duels:adminAdmin panel
