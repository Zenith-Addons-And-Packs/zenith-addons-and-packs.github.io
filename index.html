FAQ

Questions

Does it work on Realms?

Yes. There are no experiments to turn on and nothing that needs a dedicated server. Apply the pack to a world and upload it to your Realm. Install guide →

Which Bedrock version do I need?

1.26.50 or newer. Each product page lists its exact requirements.

Do the addons work together?

Yes. Install any mix and they connect on their own: one shared wallet, clan tags in FFA and Duels, event prizes in money and clan points, and one menu that links them all.

Will it break when Bedrock updates?

Our packs are built to survive updates. If one ever breaks, the fix is free during your update period (3 or 12 months), and commissions get 2 months of free support.

Can I edit the settings?

Yes. Every setting is in an in-game admin panel and saves to your world, so there are no files to edit.

Can I put my server's name on it?

Yes, with the rebrand add-on (+$2 per pack). We build it with your server's name in chat, menus and titles. The license and the "Powered by Zenith" credit stay.

Can I resell or reupload it?

No. Your license covers your own Realms and servers. Release builds are protected. Commission work is yours to use, but not to resell as a product unless we agree on it.

How do I get help?

Open a ticket in our Discord. Our team answers there. Every admin panel in-game shows the link too.

Refunds?

Digital files can't be returned, so ask anything you're unsure about before buying. If it doesn't work on a supported setup and we can't fix it, you get your money back.
