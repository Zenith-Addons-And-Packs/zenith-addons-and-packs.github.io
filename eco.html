Features

Everything money needs

Built so admins never touch a config file, and players always know where their money went.

💵Dupe-proof money

Balances are stored as data, not items, so they can't be duped, dropped or lost. Offline players keep theirs.

🛒In-game shop editor

Hold an item and press "Add item in my hand". Set buy and sell prices, and add categories. Done.

♻️Sell hand / sell all

Turn loot into money in one tap. Renamed and enchanted items are skipped, so nothing valuable goes by accident.

🤝/pay with tax

Player payments with an optional tax as a money sink. Both sides get a logged receipt.

📅Daily streak

Rewards grow every day in a row, with a streak bonus, a best-streak record and a reminder on join.

🔨Auction house

List the item you hold with a price and duration. Search, sort, buy, and get unsold items back, even offline.

⛏️Jobs

Get paid for mining ores and chopping logs. Placed blocks don't pay, so it can't be farmed. Rates set in-game.

🎯Bounties

Put money on a player's head. Whoever takes them out collects it. Minimum and fee are yours to set.

🧾History & audit log

Players see their last 20 transactions. Admins get a full audit log and can look up any player, even offline.

🗡️Kill rewards

Money for mobs (zombie to ender dragon, all editable) and for player kills, with anti-farm-spam.

🔌Command-block hooks

/scriptevent zenith_eco:give 50 from any button, NPC or parkour finish. take works too.

📊Scoreboard mirror

Balances are copied to a scoreboard objective, so your other addons and commands can read them.

🏆Live rich list

Floating top-10 boards plus a richest-players screen. Wallets show balance, rank and history.

🎨Your currency

Name it and pick a symbol: $, Gems, Coins, Shards. Set the starting balance and the max.

🛡️Exploit guards

The editor won't let a sell price reach the buy price, and sells re-check every slot at the moment of sale.

🎮Admin panel

Shop editor, give/take/set money, settings, boards and a full reset, all in-game.

Setup

Live in 1 minute

Install

Open the .mcaddon and turn on Zenith Economy. No experiments.

Name your money

/zenith_eco:admin → Settings: set the currency name, symbol and starting balance.

Stock the shop

The default shop works out of the box. Add your own items by holding them in the shop editor.

Commands

Short list, all you need

/zenith_eco:menuEverything in one menu
/zenith_eco:pay <player> <amount>Pay a player
/zenith_eco:shopBuy from the shop
/zenith_eco:sellSell hand or sell all
/zenith_eco:dailyDaily reward with streak
/zenith_eco:balance [player]Wallet and history
