Features

Everything a clan server needs

Players run their own clans from one menu. You control the rules from the admin panel.

👑Roles & invites

Leader, officers and members. Invite, kick, promote and hand over the clan from a menu.

🏷️Clan tags

Pick a 2–4 letter tag and a colour. It shows above heads, also in Zenith FFA and Duels.

💬Clan chat

Talk to your clan only with /zenith_clans:c. Online members see it instantly.

🏠Clan home

Leaders set a home. Members warp there after a short warmup, so it can't be used to escape fights.

🏦Clan bank

Members deposit money, leaders and officers withdraw. Works with Zenith Economy.

🛡️No friendly fire

Clanmates and allies can't hurt each other. Ability weapons skip them too.

⭐Levels & perks

Clans level up from points: more member slots, a bigger bank and a faster home warmup.

⚔️Clan wars

Declare war, the other leader accepts, and a timed points race begins. Winner takes bonus points.

🤝Allies

Team up with other clans. Allies can't hit each other and show in your clan info.

📜Weekly quests

Kills, points, deposits, event points and war wins, with progress bars and point rewards. New quests every Monday.

📊Leaderboards

Top clans by points, kills or K/D, in a menu and on floating boards you place.

🎮Admin panel

Member limits, costs, points, wars, bank rules and boards, all in-game. Everything saves.

Setup

Running in 2 minutes

Install

Open the .mcaddon and turn on Zenith Clans. On a Realm, upload the world. No experiments.

Make yourself admin

As an operator: /zenith_clans:setadmin @s true, then /zenith_clans:admin.

Let players in

Players open /zenith_clans:menu and create a clan. Place a clan leaderboard at spawn.

Commands

Short list, all you need

/zenith_clans:menuYour clan: create, members, wars, allies, quests, bank, home
/zenith_clans:c <message>Clan chat
/zenith_clans:invite <player>Invite a player
/zenith_clans:accept · denyAnswer an invite
/zenith_clans:homeWarp to your clan home
/zenith_clans:membersMembers with roles, online status and last seen
/zenith_clans:warDeclare, accept or check a clan war
/zenith_clans:alliesManage allied clans
/zenith_clans:questsThis week's clan quests
/zenith_clans:topTop clans
/zenith_clans:info [clan]Info on your clan or another
/zenith_clans:adminAdmin panel
