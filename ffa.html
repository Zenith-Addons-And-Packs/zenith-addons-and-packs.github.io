Features

Everything an FFA server needs

Built for open-world FFA. Players keep playing and the system runs around them.

🎒Kits with your layout

Players arrange their kit once and save it. If you change the kit later, layouts repair themselves.

🎁Rank & claim kits

Lock kits to a rank, and hand out claim kits like a VIP pack with a cooldown and a claim limit.

👑Ranks & nicknames

Owner, Admin, VIP, MVP and your own ranks with colours, shown above heads and in the kill feed.

🔥Killstreak rewards

Coins, effects or items at 3, 5, 10 and 25 kills, with a title and a server-wide shout-out. You set the steps.

⚡Kill effects

Lightning, flames, hearts, gold burst, souls, frost and more. Players pick one; you can lock them to ranks.

🎯Bounties

Streaks put coins on your head, and players can place their own. A bounty board and a floating top-bounty board.

💰Coins & shop

Dupe-proof coins from kills and streaks, a shop with categories and a daily reward. Uses the Economy wallet when installed.

🧍NPCs

Place NPCs players tap to open the menu, kits, shop, claim kits, daily reward, stats or leaderboards.

🛡️Safe spawn & combat tag

No PvP at spawn, no hiding in it mid-fight, and no /spawn or /kit in combat. Logging out in combat counts as a death.

🧱World protection

Anti-break and anti-place with an allow-list (cobwebs by default), a build height limit and a builder tag.

🚫Anti-dupe

Stops the common cursor and over-stack dupes on join and in inventories.

💤AFK & warnings

Send AFK players to spawn, kick them or just mark them. Warn rule breakers with auto-kick after three.

📊Live leaderboards

Floating boards for kills, streaks, K/D, deaths, richest and top bounties. They update the moment it changes.

🏷️Sidebar & nametags

Online count, top killer, hot streak, biggest bounty and your Discord. Rank, clan tag, HP and streak above heads.

🎮Admin panel

Spawn, kits, shop, ranks, NPCs, protection, moderation, streaks and boards, all in-game. Everything saves.

Setup

Running in 2 minutes

Install

Open the .mcaddon and turn on Zenith FFA. On a Realm, upload the world. No experiments.

Make yourself admin

As an operator: /zenith_ffa:setadmin @s true, then /zenith_ffa:admin.

Set spawn & kit

Set spawn where you stand, save your kit from your inventory, and place a leaderboard. Done.
