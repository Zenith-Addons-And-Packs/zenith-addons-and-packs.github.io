Zenith Ranked Duels

Elo-ranked 1v1: ladders, Bronze → Zenith tiers, best-of matches, seasons, spectating and auto-reset arenas.

1v1 ranked duels for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, by Zenith. Players queue from the lobby, get matched by Elo, fight in self-resetting arenas, and climb from Bronze to Zenith. Everything is set up in-game.
Version 1.2.0
Experiments None needed. Turn the pack on and play.
Game 1.26.50+
Works on Realms, single-player, hosted servers / BDS
Resource pack Not needed
Namespace zenith_duels

Features

Ladders: each has its own kit, rule and Elo

Ladder Rule
Sword Last one standing
Axe Last one standing (shield + bow)
UHC Last one standing (rod, buckets, blocks)
Boxing First to 100 hits. Nobody takes damage.
Sumo Knock your opponent off. Falling 3 blocks below the spawns loses.
Abilities Last one standing with Dash Blade, Inferno Sword and Frost Scythe. Appears automatically when Zenith Ability Weapons is installed.

Admins can re-kit any ladder from their own inventory, change Boxing's hit target, or disable ladders.

Ranked tiers & seasons

  • Elo per ladder (start 1000, K 32, K 48 for the first 10 games so new players place fast).
  • Tiers: Bronze · Silver (900) · Gold (1100) · Platinum (1300) · Diamond (1500) · Zenith (1700). Shown in menus, leaderboards and nametags, with "X to next tier" on your profile.
  • Rank up with a big title, sound and sparkles, plus a server announcement. A drop in tier gets a quiet heads-up.
  • Smart matchmaking: the allowed Elo gap starts at ±100 and widens 10 per second you wait (max ±600).
  • Anti-boosting: no Elo (and no rewards) after 3 games against the same player within an hour.
  • Seasons: end one by hand or set a season timer (e.g. 30 days). The top 3 of every ladder go into the hall of fame, everyone is soft-reset towards 1000 (you choose how much they keep), and with Zenith Economy the top 3 get season rewards ($1000 / $500 / $250 by default). The profile shows when the season ends.

Matches

  • Countdown titles with rising sounds, FIGHT!, round-won/lost titles, and Victory/Defeat screens with your Elo change.
  • Best of 3 / best of 5: a server-wide format, and players can pick their own format in duel requests.
  • Match recap after every match: hits landed, damage dealt and taken, longest combo, rounds and fight time, side by side with your opponent. Also in chat and with /zenith_duels:recap.
  • Combo counter on the HUD, a hit tick for every Boxing hit.
  • Time limit counts fighting time only (not breaks). At the limit, more rounds, then more hits / more health wins.
  • Forfeit and leaving count as a loss. A player who rejoins is cleaned up and sent back.
  • Auto-reset arenas: save a snapshot once and the arena is restored after every round. Up to 20 arenas, each limited to chosen ladders.
  • Outsiders can't interfere (arrows from the lobby do nothing). Drops never carry over between rounds.

Watch matches

  • Watch a match from the menu or /zenith_duels:watch: a live list with scores and clocks. You watch in spectator mode from above the arena, with both players' health on your screen, and go back exactly where you were afterwards.

Rematch

  • After every match both players see the recap with Rematch? for 10 seconds. If both accept, a new match starts on the same ladder and format.

Rewards (with Zenith Economy / Zenith Clans) These turn on by themselves when the other pack is installed on the same world, and are all adjustable.

  • Coins for every win: $25 ranked, $5 unranked queue.
  • Optional ranked entry fee. Refunded if the match ends before the fight starts.
  • Wagers on duel requests (up to $1000): the winner takes both stakes, a draw gives them back.
  • Clan points for the winner's clan on every ranked win (3 by default).
  • Offline season winners get paid when they next join.

Players

  • Kit editor: arrange each ladder's kit once and it's saved. Layouts adapt when an admin changes the kit.
  • Lobby hotbar: Ranked, Unranked, Kit Editor, Stats and Leaderboards. Locked in place.
  • Profile with tier and Elo per ladder, W/L, rank position, season best, win streak and match history. Leaderboards per ladder, plus past seasons.
  • HUD: opponent HP, hits, score, combo and clock in matches, search time and Elo range in the queue.
  • Rank tag and clan tag (from Zenith Clans) in nametags. Floating Elo leaderboards you can place anywhere.
  • Lobby protection: no PvP outside matches.

Setup (5 minutes)

  1. Turn on the pack (Behavior Packs → Zenith Ranked Duels).
  2. As an operator: /zenith_duels:setadmin @s true, then /zenith_duels:admin.
  3. Set lobby here while standing in your lobby.
  4. Arenas → + New arena, then:
    • Stand on each side: Set spawn A here / Set spawn B here.
    • For auto-reset: set box corner 1 and 2 around the whole arena (max 64×384×64), then Save snapshot.
    • Optional: Allowed ladders (e.g. only Sumo on your sumo platform).
  5. Build at least one arena per ladder you want live, or let arenas allow all ladders.
  6. Optional: Leaderboards → Place board here, Seasons → Season settings for a season timer, Rewards for coins and clan points.

Customization

Everything is set in-game from the admin panel and saved to the world. No files to edit.

  • Settings → Matches: countdown, format (single / best of 3 / best of 5), format choice in duel requests, time limit, rematch time, result screen time, instant respawn, recap in chat.
  • Settings → Ranked & matchmaking: K-factors, Elo range start / growth / max, same-opponent limit, rank-up announcements.
  • Settings → Lobby & display: unranked + duel requests, request timeout, lobby hotbar, lobby protection, HUD, rank + clan nametags, server type, lobby radius.
  • Rewards: coins per ranked / unranked win, ranked entry fee, max wager, clan points per ranked win.
  • Seasons: end a season now, season length (timer), how much Elo players keep, rewards for #1 / #2 / #3.

Rebrand add-on (+$2): we build the pack with your server's name in chat, menus, titles and the pack name (e.g. "RoyalMC Ranked Duels"). The license and the credit line stay with Zenith ("Powered by Zenith").

Protected code: release builds are bundled and obfuscated. Redistribution, resale or modification isn't allowed. Your license covers your own Realms/servers.

Notes

  • The lobby is Adventure mode and matches are Survival, so UHC can build. The lobby hotbar clears the inventory. Turn Lobby hotbar items off if you run Duels in a survival world.
  • Shared worlds: with Zenith FFA or Zenith Events on the same world, Duels only looks after its own lobby area (change it under Server type). Players busy in an event are never pulled into the lobby, protected or queued. If someone queues or watches from outside the lobby, their own items are saved and given back (with names, enchantments and durability), and watchers return to where they stood.
  • Entry fees and wagers need Zenith Economy's Scoreboard mirror setting (on by default).
  • "Instant respawn between rounds" sets the doImmediateRespawn game rule. Turn it off to keep the death screen.
  • Match state is in memory. After /reload or a restart, anyone mid-match is sent back and dirty arenas are reset.
