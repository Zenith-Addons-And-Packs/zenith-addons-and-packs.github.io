Zenith Events

Tournaments, Sumo, LMS, KOTH, Races and Spleef, with auto events and money, clan point and loot prizes.

Server events and tournaments for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, by Zenith. Host one from a menu, or let auto events run on their own. Players vote on what's next, join with one command, the system runs the bracket or match, and winners get paid.
Version 1.2.0
Experiments None needed. Turn the pack on and play.
Game 1.26.50+
Works on Realms, single-player, hosted servers / BDS
Namespace zenith_events

Event types

Event How it works
Tournament 1v1 single-elimination bracket with the event kit. Byes for odd numbers, a time limit per fight (more health wins), and everyone else watches from the event lobby.
Sumo Tournament The same bracket, but nobody takes damage. Knock your opponent off the platform.
Last Man Standing Everyone spawns at once with one life each. Kills are tracked. Last one alive wins.
King of the Hill Score by holding the hill alone. Contested hills don't count. Respawn on death. First to 60s wins.
Race Everyone starts together, with no PvP. First to the finish wins. Fall and you go back to the start.
Spleef Instant-break shovels, no damage, just knockback. Fall below the floor and you're out. Last one standing wins. Save an arena snapshot and the floor rebuilds itself.
Sumo Brawl Free-for-all sumo. Everyone on the platform at once, no damage, just knockback. Knocked off = out. Knock-offs are credited.
One in the Chamber A bow, a sword and one arrow. Arrows kill in one hit, every kill gives you an arrow back, missed arrows are gone. 3 lives each.

Features

  • Signups: announced in chat and as a title on everyone's screen, with the prize in the announcement. Joined players see a live countdown on their action bar.
  • Reward preview: the join menu and the join screen show exactly what 1st, 2nd and 3rd win (money, clan points, items) before you join.
  • Fair play: frozen countdowns with titles and sounds, and players outside the action can't hurt or be hurt. Outsiders can't interfere with participants, and fighters can't break the arena (except in Spleef).
  • Safe for survival worlds: joining asks before clearing the inventory. Everyone gets their game mode and position back afterwards.
  • Plays nice with Zenith Duels: players in an event are marked busy, so Duels leaves them alone, and players in a duel are asked to finish it before joining.
  • Live status: bracket view with results and upcoming fights, alive counts, kills, KOTH scores, race placings and times.
  • Spectate: once you're out (or while you wait for your next fight), pick a player to watch. You fly unseen and stay close to the action.
  • Rewards for 1st / 2nd / 3rd:
    • Money through Zenith Economy
    • Clan points through Zenith Clans
    • Reward items saved from your inventory for the winner
    • A gold particle burst with Zenith Ability Weapons
    • Only the packs you have show up in menus and results.
  • Entry fees (with Zenith Economy): optional, refunded if the event is cancelled or a player backs out before it starts. Fees can be added to the winner's prize.
  • Winner celebrations: VICTORY title and sound for the winner, place titles for 2nd and 3rd, round and FINAL titles in brackets.
  • Auto events:
    • Every N minutes after the last event, and/or at set clock times (e.g. 18:00 and 21:30 in your time zone).
    • Rotate through the event types in order, or pick at random.
    • Heads-up announcements 5 minutes, 1 minute and 10 seconds before.
  • Player vote: before each auto event, players pick between 3 event types in a pop-up screen (or /zenith_events:vote). Hosts can also start a vote any time.
  • Champions and records: wins and podiums per player, a top-10 screen and a floating Event Champions board. All-time records for fastest race, longest hill hold, most One in the Chamber kills and most Sumo Brawl knock-offs, plus the top winner of every event type.
  • Auto-reset arena: save a structure snapshot once and the fight arena is restored between fights.
  • Disconnects and /reload: players who leave mid-event are eliminated and restored when they come back.

Setup (5 minutes)

  1. As an operator: /zenith_events:setadmin @s true
  2. /zenith_events:host → Setup. Stand at each spot and press its button:
    • Event lobby (where players wait and spectate)
    • Fight spawn A / B (Tournament + Sumo Tournament), plus arena corners → Save snapshot for auto-reset
    • FFA spawns: add 4+ (Last Man Standing, KOTH, Spleef, Sumo Brawl, One in the Chamber). Spleef and Sumo Brawl need the spawns on a raised platform: falling 3 blocks below the lowest spawn means you're out.
    • Hill (KOTH), Race start and Race finish
    • Set event kit from my inventory (optional)
  3. Rewards & entry fee and Auto events & voting as you like.
  4. Start an event → pick a type, or Let players vote. Types that still need setup say what's missing.

Commands

Command Who What
/zenith_events:join · :leave Everyone Join or leave the current event
/zenith_events:menu Everyone Status, rewards, vote, spectate, event types, champions
/zenith_events:vote Everyone Vote for the next event (while a vote is open)
/zenith_events:spectate Participants Watch the action once you're out
/zenith_events:top · :records Everyone Event champions and all-time records
/zenith_events:host Hosts Host panel
/zenith_events:start <tournament|sumo|lms|koth|race|spleef|brawl|oitc> Operators, command blocks Start an event
/zenith_events:setadmin <players> <true|false> Operators Give/remove host rights

Customization

Everything is set in-game from the host panel and saved to the world. No files to edit.

Menu What you can change
Rewards & entry fee Money and clan points for 1st/2nd/3rd, entry fee, fees to the winner, the winner's reward items
Auto events & voting Interval, clock times and time zone (the menu shows the current time so you can check it), minimum players online, rotation or random, voting on/off, vote time, vote pop-up, which event types are included
Settings Signup time, countdown, fight and event time limits, KOTH target and hill size, race finish size, One in the Chamber lives, spectating on/off, title announcements
Champions board & records Place/remove the floating board, view records, reset stats

New event types (Sumo Brawl, One in the Chamber) are included in auto events on new worlds. On worlds from an older version, tick them in Auto events & voting.
