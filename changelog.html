Every update to every pack. Updates are free during your update period. Join the Discord to get notified.

🗡️ Zenith FFA

1.2.0

  • Killstreak rewards: coins, potion effects and items at the streaks you choose (3 / 5 / 10 / 25 built in), with a title and sound for the player and a server-wide shout-out. Edit them in Admin → Streaks & bounties.
  • Kill effects: 8 effects (Lightning, Flames, Hearts, Gold Burst, Souls, Explosion, Frost, Classic) that play where your victim falls. Pick one in your profile or with /zenith_ffa:effects, with a private preview. Admins lock effects to a rank or hide them.
  • Bounty board (/zenith_ffa:bounty): every bounty on pages, place your own bounty, and a Top Bounties floating board. Bounties now stay until claimed instead of vanishing when the player logs out or dies to the environment.
  • Ranks & nicknames: built-in and custom ranks in nametags and the kill feed, rank kits and claim kits by rank, zenith_rank:<id> tags (adding the tag yourself grants the rank), nicknames, and /zenith_ffa:rank and /zenith_ffa:nick.
  • Claim kits with cooldowns and claim limits (/zenith_ffa:claim).
  • NPCs for the menu, kits, claim kits, shop, daily reward, leaderboards, profile and bounty board.
  • World protection: anti-break and anti-place with an allow-list, build height limit, anti-dupe.
  • Moderation: warnings with auto-kick (/zenith_ffa:warn) and anti-AFK.
  • Zenith Economy wallet: one balance across the server when Economy is installed.
  • Shop categories, a Most Deaths leaderboard, and a profile screen that works for offline players too.
  • Plays nicely with Zenith Duels and Events: players in a duel or event are ignored by FFA's stats, streaks, bounties, spawn, kit, safe zone, AFK check and protection.
  • Settings split into pages (Combat & kits, Coins & daily, Display) with more options: streak bonus, daily per-day and streak length, show ranks.
  • Polish: success and error sounds on every action, titles for rewards and claims, confirm before buying or placing a bounty, Back buttons that return where you came from.
  • Fixes: editing the shop without Ability Weapons installed no longer deletes its weapons from the shop; buying with a full inventory no longer loses the item; claim kits check for free slots first; the safe zone now cancels hits (including arrows) instead of healing them back; AFK players sent to spawn are no longer welcomed back instantly; nicknames can't copy another player's name; tapping an NPC twice quickly no longer opens two menus; build limit checks the exact block being placed; faster nametags and leaderboards on busy servers.

1.1.0

  • Arena resets: corner-to-corner fills with block choice, replace-only filter, blocks-per-fill and tick-delay control, auto timer, warnings, evacuation.
  • A support line in the admin panel.

1.0.0

  • First release.

⚔️ Zenith Ranked Duels

1.2.0

  • Rank-up celebrations with title, sound and sparkles. Tier names and "to next tier" in every menu.
  • Seasons: season timer, adjustable soft reset, season rewards with Zenith Economy, season countdown on the profile.
  • Match recap (hits, damage, longest combo, fight time) after every match, in the rematch screen and with /zenith_duels:recap.
  • Best of 3 / 5 per challenge: duel requests pick their own format, and can carry a wager.
  • Rewards: coins for wins, optional ranked entry fee, clan points with Zenith Clans.
  • Watch a match from a live list; watchers go back where they were with their items.
  • Clan tags in nametags. Countdown, round and Boxing hit sounds. Combo counter on the HUD.
  • Admin panel: settings in groups, Rewards page, watch or end any live match.
  • Fixes: the time limit no longer counts breaks between rounds; watching a match no longer wipes your inventory; players can't force a draw on someone else's match with /forfeit while watching; a player leaving between rounds can't restart the match; your duel kit is no longer kept after a match when the lobby hotbar is off; Duels leaves players in Zenith Events alone and treats Events like FFA on shared worlds; Duels no longer overwrites other packs' nametags outside its lobby.

1.1.0

  • Rematch prompt after every match.
  • A support line in the admin panel.

💰 Zenith Economy

1.2.0

  • Auction house: list, browse, search & sort, buy, cancel, expiry with a claim box, sales tax and listing limits.
  • Jobs: pay for mining ores and chopping logs, with an in-game rate editor. Player-placed blocks don't pay.
  • Mob rewards editor, and bounty minimum and fee in Settings.
  • Daily login streak now works by calendar day (pick when the day starts), counts beyond day 7, shows your best streak, pays a streak bonus and reminds you on join.
  • Transaction history screen (last 20), player lookup for admins (offline too), and an audit log.
  • Confirm step for big payments and shop buys; every purchase shows the total and balance after. Sell all of this type and /zenith_eco:sellall with a breakdown.
  • New script events: give_silent, take_silent and set.
  • Settings split into sections; new options for confirmations, kill cooldown, daily streak and auction house.
  • Fixes: a bounty is no longer lost when the killer's balance is full; player kill rewards can't be farmed with alt accounts (per-victim cooldown); no kill rewards for players busy in a duel or event; selling with a full balance no longer deletes the items; negative amounts show as -$5; editing the shop no longer drops hidden Ability Weapons items; faster selling and saving on busy servers.

1.1.0

  • Player bounties (place, stack, claim on kill, top list).
  • A support line in the admin panel.

1.0.0

  • First release.

🏆 Zenith Events

1.2.0

  • Two new events: Sumo Brawl (free-for-all sumo) and One in the Chamber.
  • Player vote: pick the next event from 3 options in a pop-up screen or with /zenith_events:vote. Hosts can start a vote from the host panel.
  • Auto events upgrade: clock times with a time zone, rotation in order, and heads-up announcements 5 min / 1 min / 10 s before.
  • Reward preview in the join menu and join screen, and prizes shown in signups and results.
  • Entry fees with refunds when an event is cancelled, optionally added to the winner's prize.
  • Spectate (/zenith_events:spectate) for players who are out or waiting for their fight.
  • Records (/zenith_events:records): fastest race, longest hill hold, most kills, most knock-offs, most wins per event type.
  • Countdown, round, FINAL, VICTORY and podium titles with sounds. Kill counts in Last Man Standing.
  • Works with Zenith Duels: event players are marked busy, and players in a duel can't join until it's over.
  • Money and clan point rewards only apply (and only show up) when Zenith Economy / Zenith Clans are installed.
  • Fixes: players who left a tournament could be pulled into a later fight; leavers could get podium rewards; "Clear saved reward items" didn't work; the Refresh button in Live status closed the screen; fighters could break the arena; players coming back after a disconnect stayed in the arena instead of being restored.

1.1.0

  • Spleef event (works with auto events and the arena snapshot).
  • A support line in the host panel.

1.0.0

  • First release: 5 event types, auto events, rewards, champions board.
