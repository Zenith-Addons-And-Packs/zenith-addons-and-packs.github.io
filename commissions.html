How it works

Five clear steps

Tell us the idea

Open a ticket in Discord. Describe what you want, your platform (Realm/server) and any deadline.

Get a scope & quote

You get a numbered feature list (what's included and what isn't), a price, revisions and milestones.

Good to know

Commission FAQ

What can you build?

Pretty much anything Bedrock can run: game modes (FFA, duels, bedwars-style, events), economies and shops, ranks via tags, custom items with abilities and particles, UIs and admin panels, leaderboards, anti-cheat helpers, and integrations with our packs.

Realm or server?

Both. By default everything is Realm-safe with no experiments. If you want experimental or server-only features, we'll explain the trade-offs in the quote.

Do I get the source code?

You get a protected release build by default. If you need the readable source, ask before we quote. It's priced as a source license.

Rush jobs?

Under 48 hours: +30%, if the schedule allows.

How do I pay?

PayPal invoice (Goods & Services) in USD. Never pay anyone in DMs who claims to be us.

Open a ticket and describe it. A quote usually comes within a day.
