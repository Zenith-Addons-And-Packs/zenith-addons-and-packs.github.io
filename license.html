License & terms

Clear and fair.

The short version: use it on your own Realms and servers, don't share or resell it, and we'll keep it working.

1. What you get

Buying a Zenith pack gives you a non-exclusive, non-transferable license to use it on Realms and servers that you own or run. Players on those Realms/servers can of course play with it.

2. What's not allowed

  • Sharing, uploading, reselling or giving away the files (whole or in part), including to friends or other server owners.
  • Removing or bypassing the protection, decompiling, or modifying the code.
  • Claiming the pack as your own work, or removing the Zenith credit line.

3. Rebrand add-on (+$2 per pack)

With the rebrand add-on, we build the pack with your server's name in chat, menus, titles and the pack name. The license stays with Zenith and the in-game credit reads "Powered by Zenith". Rebranded builds fall under the same license.

4. Updates

Standard includes 3 months of updates and Premium 12 months, counted from purchase. That includes fixes for Bedrock updates. After that, updates are optional paid upgrades.

5. Refunds

Digital products can't be returned, so ask anything before buying. If a pack doesn't work on a supported setup (Bedrock 1.26.50+, as described on its page) and we can't fix it, you get a full refund.

6. Support

Support is through Discord tickets. Include your platform, game version and (for errors) a screenshot of the content log.

7. Payments

Payments are only through PayPal invoices sent by Zenith staff in a ticket. We never ask for payment in DMs.

8. Commissions

Commission work is licensed to the client for their own use. Resale as a product needs written agreement. Scope, price, revisions and milestones are agreed in writing (in the ticket) before work starts.

9. Not affiliated

Zenith is not an official Minecraft product and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.

Questions?

Ask before you buy. We're happy to help.
