Features

Eight modes, zero effort

Everything runs itself: signups, brackets, countdowns, results and rewards.

⚔️Tournament

1v1 single elimination with byes, fight timers and a live bracket for spectators.

🟢Sumo Tournament

The same bracket, no damage. Knock them off the platform.

💀Last Man Standing

Everyone at once, one life each. Last one alive wins.

👑King of the Hill

Hold the hill alone to score. Contested doesn't count. First to 60s.

❄️Spleef

Instant-break shovels, no damage. Dig the floor out from under everyone. The arena rebuilds itself after.

🏁Race

Parkour or obstacle race. No PvP, fall and restart, top 3 rewarded.

🌀Sumo Brawl

Free-for-all sumo on one platform. Last one standing on the stage wins.

🏹One in the Chamber

One arrow that kills, a few lives each. Get a kill to get your arrow back.

⏰Scheduler

Every N minutes or at set times of day, in rotation or random, with warnings at 5 min, 1 min and 10 s.

🗳️Player votes

Before each auto event, players pick the mode from three options in a pop-up.

🎁Real rewards

Money (Zenith Economy), clan points (Zenith Clans) and loot for 1st, 2nd and 3rd, shown before players join.

🎟️Entry fees

Optional fee to join, refunded if the event is cancelled. Add the pot to the winner's prize.

📢Hype built in

Title announcements, countdowns, a podium broadcast and a gold burst for the winner.

🏆Champions & records

Wins, podiums and all-time records per mode, a top-10 screen and a floating leaderboard.

👀Spectate

Players who are out can watch the rest of the event from a spectate menu.

🔁Auto-reset arena

Save a snapshot once and the fight arena is restored between fights.

🛡️Survival-safe

Joining asks before clearing the inventory. Everyone gets their spot and game mode back.

🔌Disconnect-proof

Leavers are eliminated and restored when they rejoin. Survives /reload.

Setup

Live in 5 minutes

Install

Open the .mcaddon and turn on Zenith Events. No experiments.

Set spots

/zenith_events:host → Setup: lobby, fight spawns, FFA spawns, hill, race start and finish.

Go live

Press Start, or turn on the scheduler and let the server run them with player votes.

Commands

Short list, all you need

/zenith_events:joinJoin the current event
/zenith_events:menuStatus, bracket, event types, champions
/zenith_events:hostHost panel
/zenith_events:voteVote for the next event
/zenith_events:spectateWatch the current event
/zenith_events:recordsAll-time records per mode
/zenith_events:start <type>For command blocks: tournament, sumo, brawl, lms, koth, oitc, race, spleef
