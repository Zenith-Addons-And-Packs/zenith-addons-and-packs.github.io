Customize & optimize

Your server, your rules

⚒️The Forge

Per weapon: on/off, cooldown, power 25–200%, damage multiplier and effect color. Give weapons at any level.

⬆️Upgrade levels

Players upgrade weapons from I to V: more power and shorter cooldowns. Pay with Economy money or XP levels.

⏳Cooldown bar

A clean bar and countdown on the action bar while holding a weapon, with a ping when it's ready.

💥Hit & kill feedback

Hit counter, chime, KO burst and ability kill-streak titles. Rate-limited, so it never spams.

🎨Player colors

Players pick their own effect color in the Armory: gold, ember, crimson, cyan, violet and more.

🎯Who gets hit

Players, mobs or both. Clanmates, allies and pets are skipped, and no-ability zones keep hubs calm.

📊Weapon leaderboard

Casts, player kills and mob kills per weapon, in a leaderboard players can open.

🚀No per-tick loops

Abilities run on events and use the game's native cooldowns. Nothing scans every player every tick.

📉Particle quality

Low, Medium or High. Scales every effect for big servers and low-end phones.

🧯Cast cap

A server-wide limit on casts per second. Spam can't lag your server, and failed casts don't use the cooldown.

Better together

Works with every Zenith pack

Ranked Duels gets an Abilities ladder with its own Elo. Economy and FFA add the weapons to their shops, and kills get a gold burst. It all switches on automatically when the packs are installed together.
