Zenith FFA

Open-world FFA core: kit layouts, coins, bounties, killstreak rewards, ranks, NPCs, anti-dupe and live leaderboards.

An open-world free-for-all PvP core for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, by Zenith. Players spawn, grab their kit in their own layout, and fight. Kills earn coins, streaks unlock rewards and put bounties on heads, and coins buy gear in the shop. Ranks, nicknames, NPCs, world protection and moderation are built in. You set everything up in-game, with no command blocks and no experiments.
Version 1.2.0
Experiments None needed. Turn the pack on and play.

Features

Kits

  • One main kit, saved from your own inventory (enchants, names and lore kept). Rank kits are locked to a rank or tag such as vip.
  • Personal layouts: players arrange their kit once and save it. If an admin changes the kit later, layouts repair themselves: items keep their slots and new items fill free ones.
  • Claim kits: one-off bundles (e.g. a VIP pack) with a cooldown in hours and an optional claim limit. Players need enough free slots, so nothing is lost.
  • Auto kit on respawn, plus per-kit kill rewards (golden apple, arrows).

Combat

  • Kill feed showing the killer's hearts. Void, lava and fall kills go to the last player who hit you.
  • Combat tag (12s): /kit, /spawn and /shop are blocked while in combat. Logging out in combat counts as a death and gives the kill away.
  • Heal on kill, cleared drops, no hunger. Streak announcements at milestones you choose.
  • Killstreak rewards: reach a streak and get bonus coins, a potion effect and/or an item. Built-in: 3 kills (+10 coins), 5 (+20, Speed I), 10 (+50, Strength I), 25 (+150, Regeneration II, 3 golden apples). Add, edit or remove any streak. The player gets a big title and sound, and the whole server sees the shout-out.
  • Kill effects: players pick a burst that plays where their victim falls: Classic, Lightning, Flames, Hearts, Gold Burst, Souls, Explosion, Frost (or None). Lock any effect to a rank (that rank and higher ranks can use it) or hide it. Lightning starts locked to MVP and Gold Burst to VIP.

Coins (stored as data, not an item, so they can't be duped, dropped or lost)

  • Coins per kill, plus a streak bonus.
  • Revenge bonus for killing the player who last killed you.
  • Daily reward with a 7-day login streak.
  • Shop with categories: items and potion effects. Admins add whatever they're holding, or any effect, and can file each entry under a category. If your inventory is full, buying an item is refused before you pay.
  • One wallet with Zenith Economy: when Economy is on the same world, FFA pays into and spends from the Economy balance, and Economy's daily reward and bounties are used.

Bounties

  • Every 5-kill streak puts coins on your head. Players can place their own bounties too (minimum 25 coins, you choose).
  • Bounties stay until someone claims them. Logging out or dying to lava doesn't clear yours. Whoever kills you collects all of it.
  • Bounty board: every bounty, highest first, across as many pages as needed, with online/offline status and a tap-through to each player's profile.
  • Bounties show in nametags, the sidebar, and on a Top Bounties floating board.

Ranks & nicknames

  • 8 built-in ranks (Owner, Admin, Mod, Helper, Media, Legend, MVP, VIP). Create your own with colors and a weight (higher shows first). Up to 3 ranks per player.
  • Ranks show in nametags and the kill feed, unlock rank kits, claim kits and kill effects, and add the tag zenith_rank:<id> so other packs and command blocks can read them. Adding that tag with a command (from a store, for example) gives the rank on the player's next join.
  • Nicknames (letters, numbers, spaces, . - _). Nobody can take another player's name.
  • Ranks show above heads, not in chat: Minecraft doesn't let add-ons change chat messages without experiments.

Spawn

  • Safe zone around spawn: no PvP for untagged players, melee or arrows. Anyone who runs to spawn mid-fight stays hittable, and nobody can shoot out of the zone.
  • /spawn with a 5-second stand-still warmup that cancels if you move or get hit.

NPCs

  • Place NPCs that open the FFA menu, kits, claim kits, shop, daily reward, leaderboards, profile or bounty board. Players can't hurt or edit them.

World protection

  • Anti-break and anti-place with an allow-list (tap a block to allow it; cobwebs are allowed by default).
  • Build height limit.
  • Anti-dupe: blocks the disconnect cursor dupe and trims over-stacked items.
  • Builders (tag zenith_ffa:builder) and creative players can always build.

Moderation

  • Warnings with reasons, kept per player (last 10). Players see a big warning title. Auto-kick at a number of warnings you choose.
  • Anti-AFK: after N seconds without moving, send to spawn, kick, or just mark them [AFK], with a warning title first. Players in combat never count as AFK; tag zenith_ffa:afk_bypass skips the check.

Display

  • Info sidebar: online count, top killer, hottest live streak, biggest bounty, and your Discord and address lines.
  • Nametags showing rank, clan tag (with Zenith Clans), HP, streak and bounty.
  • Action-bar HUD showing coins, streak, K/D, and a combat timer or "Safe" indicator.
  • Floating leaderboards you place anywhere: Top Kills, Best Streaks, Best K/D, Most Deaths, Richest, Top Bounties. They update live.
  • Player profile: kills, deaths, K/D, best and current streak, coins, bounty, ranks and leaderboard place, for you or anyone (online or offline). Your own profile is where you pick your kill effect.
  • A "+1 KILL · +12 coins" title on every kill.

Arena resets

  • Set corner 1 and corner 2, choose what to fill (any block, or the block in your hand), and optionally replace only certain blocks. The default clears player-placed cobblestone, webs, lava, water and fire, and never touches your map.
  • Big arenas never lag: the reset is cut into fills of N blocks (256–32,768) with N ticks between them. The panel shows how many fills it takes and how long.
  • Auto-reset every N minutes with countdown warnings in chat. Optionally move players inside to spawn first. Up to 10 zones, and dropped items are cleared too.

Works with other Zenith packs

  • Zenith Economy: one shared wallet (see Coins).
  • Zenith Duels and Zenith Events: players in a duel or event are left alone. FFA doesn't count their kills, deaths, streaks or bounties, doesn't send them to FFA spawn or give them the kit, and skips the safe zone, AFK check and protection for them.
  • Zenith Clans: clan tags show in FFA nametags.
  • Zenith Ability Weapons: four weapons appear in the coin shop, and the Classic and Gold Burst kill effects get a gold particle burst.
  • The FFA menu links to each of these packs' menus when they're installed.

All data survives /reload, relogs and restarts. Admins edit everything in-game.

Setup (2 minutes)

  1. Turn on the pack (Behavior Packs → Zenith FFA).
  2. As an operator: /zenith_ffa:setadmin @s true
  3. /zenith_ffa:admin:
    • Spawn & safe zone: stand at spawn and tick "Set spawn to where I'm standing".
    • Kits: wear and hold your kit, then "Set main kit from my inventory".
    • Leaderboards: place a board or two.
    • Settings → Sidebar, nametags & HUD: add your Discord and address to the sidebar.
  4. Optional: give staff their ranks (Ranks & nicknames), place NPCs at spawn (NPCs), and check Protection before players arrive.
  5. Done. Players use /zenith_ffa:menu.

Kit, claim, shop and spawn are blocked in combat (you can turn this off) and for players in a duel or event.

Admin panel

  • Spawn & safe zone: set spawn, safe zone radius, warmup, respawn and join behavior.
  • Kits: main kit, rank kits, claim kits, preview, reset.
  • Shop: sell the held item or an effect (with a category), remove, reset.
  • Leaderboards: place any board type, remove the nearest, refresh.
  • Streaks & bounties: add, edit and delete killstreak rewards (coins, effect, held item); reward announcements; streak milestones; streak bounty size and frequency; player bounties and minimum; remove one bounty or all.
  • Kill effects: turn them on or off, lock each effect to a rank or hide it, unlock all.
  • Arena resets: zones, corners, fill block, replace-only list, fill size and speed, auto timer, warnings, evacuation.
  • Protection: anti-break/place, allow-list, build limit, anti-dupe, builder mode.
  • Ranks & nicknames: give, take, nickname, create/edit, delete, reset.
  • NPCs: place any NPC type, remove the nearest.
  • Moderation: warn, view and clear warnings, kick limit, AFK time, action and warning.
  • Settings: Combat & kits, Coins & daily reward, Sidebar/nametags/HUD, reset all.
  • Players: give/take coins, reset all stats and bounties.

Notes

  • The sidebar uses the zenith_ffa:sidebar objective. Turn it off in Settings if you use the sidebar for something else.
  • Nametags replace the player's name tag text. Turn them off to show just rank, clan tag and name.
  • Floating leaderboards are a pack entity (zenith_ffa:text). If one doesn't appear, reopen the world once after adding the pack.
  • NPCs use the game's own NPC. Remove one from Admin → NPCs while standing next to it.
  • Kill effects show for everyone nearby. If your server is very busy, lock the bigger ones (Explosion, Lightning) to a rank or hide them.
