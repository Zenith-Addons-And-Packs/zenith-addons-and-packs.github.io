Zenith Economy

Server economy: dupe-proof money, a shop you edit in-game, sell all, daily streaks, bounties and hooks for every Zenith addon.

A complete server economy for Minecraft Bedrock Edition, by Zenith. Balances, payments, a category shop you edit in-game, an auction house, jobs, a daily login streak, mob and kill rewards, bounties, and hooks so command blocks and other addons can use the money.
Version 1.2.0
Experiments None needed. Turn the pack on and play.
Game 1.26.50+
Works on Realms, single-player, hosted servers / BDS
Resource pack Not needed
Namespace zenith_eco

Features

Money

  • Balances stored as world data (not items), so they can't be duped. Offline players keep theirs.
  • Custom currency name and symbol ($1,250 or 1,250 Gems), starting balance and max balance.
  • /pay with an optional tax % as a money sink. Big payments ask "are you sure?" first (you pick the amount).
  • Amounts can be typed as 1500, 1,500, 1.5k or 2m.
  • Wallet with balance, rank, all-time earnings, daily streak and auction status.
  • Transaction history: the last 20 money movements per player, with what and when. Mining and mob kills are grouped so they don't flood it.
  • Richest players list, plus floating rich-list boards that update live.

Auction house

  • List the item in your hand with a price and a time (1 hour to 7 days, you set the max). Stacks can be split.
  • Browse with pages, search by item, enchantment or seller, and sort by newest, price or ending soon.
  • Listings show enchantments, durability, lore and dye color. Buying asks to confirm and shows your balance after.
  • Sales tax % (taken from the seller's earnings) and a max listings per player.
  • Unsold items come back when the time runs out. Offline sellers, and players with a full inventory, get them in a claim box that empties into their inventory when they next join. Sellers who were offline get a "while you were away" summary.
  • Items are copied exactly. Things that can't be (potions, written books, filled shulker boxes and bundles, maps, goat horns, items with special data such as Ability Weapons) are refused with a clear message.

Shop

  • Categories (Blocks, Food, Combat, Ores & Loot by default) with buy prices and optional sell prices.
  • Buy 1–64 at once with a slider. Every purchase plays a sound and shows the total and your balance after. Big buys ask to confirm. Overflow drops at your feet, so nothing is lost.
  • Sell hand, sell all of this type and sell everything (with a breakdown before you confirm). Renamed, enchanted, worn and special items are skipped, so loot and gear can't be dumped by accident.
  • Admins add whatever they're holding to any category, reprice or remove items, and add or delete categories. The editor refuses a sell price at or above the buy price and warns when another entry would let players loop money.
  • Global sell multiplier for events.
  • With Zenith Ability Weapons installed, an Ability Weapons category ($1,500–$3,200) appears automatically. Items from packs that aren't installed are hidden.

Earning

  • Jobs: money for mining ores (coal $1 … diamond $10, emerald $12, ancient debris $25) and chopping logs ($1). Earnings pop up on the action bar. Blocks players placed themselves never pay, and creative mode doesn't pay.
  • Mob kill rewards (zombie $2 … wither $500, ender dragon $1,000) with anti-farm-spam. Every amount is editable in-game, and you can add mobs from other addons.
  • Player kill reward with a cooldown per victim, so friends and alt accounts can't farm each other.
  • Players in a duel or event from another Zenith addon don't get kill rewards from Economy (bounties still pay).
  • Daily login streak: claim once a day and the reward rises each day in a row (day 1 $50, +$10 per day, up to day 7), with a streak bonus at the end of every full week. Miss a day and it starts over. Players get a reminder when they join.

Bounties

  • Put money on any player's head: /zenith_eco:bounty <player> <amount> or Menu → Bounties. Bounties stack, are announced to the server, and whoever kills the target collects them all. A fee (10% by default) works as a money sink.

For server owners

  • Admin panel with player lookup (online and offline): give, take or set money with a reason, and view anyone's history.
  • Audit log: every admin money change, setting, shop or table edit, and command-set balance, with who and when.
  • Scoreboard mirror: balances are copied to the objective zenith_eco:money, so command blocks and other addons can read them.
  • Script events: let command blocks, NPCs and buttons give, take or set money:
    /execute as @p run scriptevent zenith_eco:give 50 Parkour reward
/execute as @p run scriptevent zenith_eco:give_silent 50 Hidden reward
/execute as @p run scriptevent zenith_eco:take 100 Entry fee
/execute as @p run scriptevent zenith_eco:take_silent 100 Entry fee
/execute as @p run scriptevent zenith_eco:set 0 Season reset
    give shows an action-bar popup; give_silent doesn't. take tells the player when they can't afford it; take_silent stays quiet. set is written to the audit log.
  • New players get a welcome message and their starting balance.

Setup (1 minute)

  1. Turn on the pack (Behavior Packs → Zenith Economy).
  2. As an operator: /zenith_eco:setadmin @s true
  3. /zenith_eco:admin:
    • Settings: currency, paying, kill rewards, daily streak, bounties, auction house, shop and display.
    • Shop editor: adjust prices, add your own items.
    • Mob rewards and Jobs: set what each mob and block pays.
    • Rich-list boards: place one at spawn.

Notes

  • In commands, put the scoreboard objective in quotes: scoreboard players test @p "zenith_eco:money" 100.
  • Watch prices across categories. If one entry sells an item for more than another entry charges for it, players can loop the two for profit. The shop editor warns you when this happens.
  • Auction items keep their name, lore, enchantments, durability and dye color. Armor trims and shield patterns aren't kept; sellers see a note before listing those.
  • Jobs remember the last 10,000 blocks players placed (ores and logs), so building with logs and breaking them again doesn't pay.
  • Zenith FFA, Clans and Events pay into and spend from this wallet when both packs are on the same world.
